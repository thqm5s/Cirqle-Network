FAQ

How does the selection work? +
Our team analyzes your journey and, most importantly, the current state of your project. We look for profiles that "do" more than they "say".
Is it free? +
Access to the platform is free for selected members. We prioritize network quality over direct access monetization.
Do I need a launched company? +
Not necessarily. You must have a concrete project under development (MVP, prototype, active research). Ideas alone are not enough.
How long does the process take? +
Every application is reviewed within 7 business days. You will receive a personalized response.
Can I be sponsored by a member? +
Yes, a current member can endorse your application. This ensures a priority review of your profile.
